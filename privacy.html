Privacy notice: the waitlist and survey

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This notice covers this website and its waitlist form. The Kintender app will have its own privacy policy, published before it launches. The app is designed so that your parent's information stays on your phone.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Your country, and your province or state if you give it
  • If you choose to answer the survey: your answers about how you help care for a parent today, which features you'd use and which plan you'd choose

We never ask for names, health details or copies of documents. Please don't include any in your answers.

Why we collect it

  • To email you once to confirm that you've signed up, and again when Kintender is ready
  • To decide what to build first, and whether to build it

We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers or use it for anything else. This website uses no cookies, analytics or ads. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure.

Where it's kept

The form is run by Tally, a form service based in Belgium, which stores and processes the answers on our behalf. Your answers are also copied to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in Kintender's Google account, which sends the confirmation email. Google stores it on our behalf. Your information may therefore be stored outside your province or country. Apart from Tally and Google, only the Kintender team can see it.

How long we keep it

Until six months after the app launches, then we delete it. If it hasn't launched by December 31, 2027, we delete it then. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices

Joining is optional, and so is the survey: you can stop after your email and country. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing support.kintender@gmail.com. Every email we send also lets you unsubscribe.

Who is responsible

The person responsible for protecting your personal information at Kintender is its privacy officer. Write to support.kintender@gmail.com with any question or complaint.
