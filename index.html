What Kintender does

  • Snap a document

    Take a photo of a letter, bill or prescription. Kintender says what it means, finds the dates and amounts, and suggests what to do next.

  • Keep it all in one place

    Your parent's medications, doctors, insurance and documents, easy to search.

  • Never miss a deadline

    Reminders for refills, appointments and appeal deadlines.

  • Get more from appointments

    Questions to ask before the visit and, with everyone's consent, a recording and a summary afterwards.

  • Be ready for an emergency

    Medications, conditions and contacts on one screen, also on your lock screen.

  • Keep the family informed

    A weekly update to send to your brothers and sisters by text or email.

Private by design

Your parent's medical paperwork never leaves your phone.

  • No account to create and no cloud. The app doesn't even connect to the internet.
  • The AI that reads the documents runs on your phone, not on a server.
  • You make the backup: a file you keep wherever you choose.

On recent phones, the explanations come from the AI built into the phone (Apple Intelligence or Gemini Nano). Other phones get a simpler version that still finds the dates, amounts and phone numbers.

It explains. It doesn't advise.

Kintender tells you what a document says, always next to the original, so you can check it. It never gives medical, legal or financial advice: for that, ask the doctor, the pharmacist or the plan.
